# ACC/AHA 2020 CLINICAL GUIDELINE CRITERIA & HEMODYNAMIC STAGING

### Aortic Stenosis (AS) ACC/AHA Staging & Doppler Criteria
- **Stage Classification (2020 ACC/AHA Guideline):**
  - **Stage A (At Risk):** Risk factors for calcific VHD (bicuspid valve, age, smoking, hypertension). Normal transvalvular hemodynamics (Vmax < 2.0 m/s).
  - **Stage B (Progressive AS):** Mild-to-moderate leaflet calcification with reduced systolic excursion.
    - Mild AS: Vmax 2.0–2.9 m/s, Mean Gradient (ΔP) < 20 mmHg.
    - Moderate AS: Vmax 3.0–3.9 m/s, Mean Gradient (ΔP) 20–39 mmHg.
  - **Stage C (Asymptomatic Severe AS):**
    - High-Gradient Severe AS: Peak Jet Velocity (Vmax) >= 4.0 m/s, Mean Transvalvular Gradient (ΔP) >= 40 mmHg, Aortic Valve Area (AVA) <= 1.0 cm² (Indexed AVA <= 0.6 cm²/m²). Left Ventricular Ejection Fraction (LVEF) >= 50% (Stage C1) or LVEF < 50% (Stage C2).
    - Low-Flow, Low-Gradient Severe AS with Reduced LVEF: AVA <= 1.0 cm², Vmax < 4.0 m/s, ΔP < 40 mmHg, LVEF < 50%, Stroke Volume Index (SVI) < 35 mL/m². Requires Dobutamine Stress Echocardiography to distinguish true severe AS from pseudo-severe AS.
    - Paradoxical Low-Flow, Low-Gradient Severe AS with Preserved LVEF: AVA <= 1.0 cm², Vmax < 4.0 m/s, ΔP < 40 mmHg, LVEF >= 50%, SVI < 35 mL/m².
  - **Stage D (Symptomatic Severe AS):** Meets severe hemodynamic criteria (Vmax >= 4.0 m/s or mean ΔP >= 40 mmHg, AVA <= 1.0 cm²) in the presence of cardinal clinical symptoms: Exertional Dyspnea, Decreased Exercise Tolerance, Angina, Presyncope, or Syncope.

### Aortic Regurgitation (AR) ACC/AHA Staging & Doppler Criteria
- **Stage Classification (2020 ACC/AHA Guideline):**
  - **Stage A (At Risk):** Bicuspid aortic valve, aortic root dilation (>4.0 cm), history of infective endocarditis.
  - **Stage B (Progressive Mild-to-Moderate AR):** Early diastolic murmur, Jet width < 65% of LVOT, Vena contracta < 0.6 cm.
  - **Stage C (Asymptomatic Severe AR):**
    - Quantitative Echocardiographic Hemodynamics:
      - Vena Contracta width > 0.6 cm.
      - Regurgitant Volume (RVol) >= 60 mL/beat.
      - Regurgitant Fraction (RF) >= 50%.
      - Effective Regurgitant Orifice Area (EROA) >= 0.30 cm².
      - Pressure Half-Time (PHT) < 200 ms (rapid equalization of aortic and LV diastolic pressures).
      - Holodiastolic flow reversal in proximal abdominal aorta.
    - Left Ventricular Remodeling: Stage C1 (LVEF >= 55%, LV end-systolic dimension LVESD <= 50 mm); Stage C2 (LVEF < 55% or LVESD > 50 mm or indexed LVESD > 25 mm/m²).
  - **Stage D (Symptomatic Severe AR):** Meets severe echocardiographic criteria accompanied by exertional dyspnea, orthopnea, angina, or overt heart failure.

### Mitral Regurgitation (MR) ACC/AHA Staging & Doppler Criteria
- **Stage Classification (Primary/Degenerative MR per 2020 ACC/AHA):**
  - **Stage A (At Risk):** Mitral valve prolapse (MVP), myxomatous degeneration without significant regurgitant jet.
  - **Stage B (Progressive MR):** Central jet 20–40% of LA area, Vena contracta < 0.7 cm, Regurgitant Volume < 60 mL, EROA < 0.40 cm².
  - **Stage C (Asymptomatic Severe MR):**
    - Quantitative Echocardiographic Hemodynamics:
      - Vena Contracta width >= 0.7 cm.
      - Regurgitant Volume (RVol) >= 60 mL/beat.
      - Regurgitant Fraction (RF) >= 50%.
      - Effective Regurgitant Orifice Area (EROA) >= 0.40 cm².
      - Systolic flow reversal in pulmonary veins.
    - Ventricular Remodeling: Stage C1 (LVEF > 60% and LVESD < 40 mm); Stage C2 (LVEF <= 60% or LVESD >= 40 mm signifying incipient LV decompensation).
  - **Stage D (Symptomatic Severe MR):** Severe MR accompanied by NYHA Functional Class II–IV symptoms.

### Mitral Stenosis (MS) ACC/AHA Staging & Doppler Criteria
- **Stage Classification (Rheumatic MS per 2020 ACC/AHA):**
  - **Stage A (At Risk):** Rheumatic valve morphology with commissural fusion, normal transmitral velocity.
  - **Stage B (Progressive MS):** Mitral Valve Area (MVA) > 1.5 cm², Diastolic Pressure Half-Time < 150 ms.
  - **Stage C (Asymptomatic Severe MS):**
    - Mitral Valve Area (MVA) <= 1.5 cm² (very severe if MVA <= 1.0 cm²).
    - Diastolic Pressure Half-Time (PHT) >= 150 ms (PHT >= 220 ms if MVA <= 1.0 cm²).
    - Mean Transmitral Gradient at rest > 5–10 mmHg.
    - Pulmonary Artery Systolic Pressure (PASP) > 30 mmHg at rest (> 50 mmHg with exercise).
  - **Stage D (Symptomatic Severe MS):** Severe MS (MVA <= 1.5 cm²) with exertional dyspnea, fatigue, orthopnea, hemoptysis, or pulmonary edema.

### Physiological / Normal Cardiac Auscultation ACC/AHA Criteria
- **Normal Hemodynamic Criteria per ACC/AHA Benchmarks:**
  - **Aortic Valve:** Peak transvalvular velocity < 1.7 m/s, mean systolic gradient < 5 mmHg, aortic valve area 3.0–4.0 cm².
  - **Mitral Valve:** Mitral valve area 4.0–6.0 cm², mean diastolic gradient < 2 mmHg, normal leaflet coaptation without regurgitation.
  - **Left Ventricle:** Preserved ejection fraction (LVEF 55%–70%), normal end-systolic dimension (LVESD < 40 mm).
  - **Valvular Regurgitation:** Absence of pathological regurgitation; trace physiological tricuspid or pulmonary regurgitation is considered benign.
